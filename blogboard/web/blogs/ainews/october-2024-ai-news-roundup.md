## 1. OpenAI Unveils **GPT‑4 Turbo 2.0**  

OpenAI announced an incremental upgrade to its flagship model, **GPT‑4 Turbo 2.0**, at the *DevDay* livestream on 9 Oct. The new engine promises:

| Feature | What’s New |
|---------|------------|
| **Speed** | Up to **2× faster** inference on the same hardware, cutting latency for chat‑based apps to sub‑200 ms. |
| **Cost** | **30 % lower** per‑token pricing for the API, making large‑scale deployments more economical. |
| **Context Window** | Expanded to **128 k tokens** (≈ 75 pages), enabling deeper document analysis and longer conversations. |
| **Safety** | Integrated “**Steer‑Guard**” layers that reduce hallucinations by ~ 15 % in benchmark tests. |

OpenAI also opened **beta access** to the new model for select partners, including Microsoft Copilot and Zapier. The rollout will be **global** by early 2025.  

*Source: OpenAI Blog – “Introducing GPT‑4 Turbo 2.0” (2024‑10‑09) – https://openai.com/blog/gpt‑4‑turbo‑2‑0*

## 2. Google DeepMind Launches **Gemini 1.5 Pro**  

Google’s AI division released **Gemini 1.5 Pro**, a multimodal model that can process text, images, audio, and video in a single prompt. Highlights:

* **Unified Reasoning** – First‑generation LLM that can answer a question about a video clip while simultaneously referencing a PDF document.  
* **Efficiency** – 40 % lower carbon footprint per inference compared with Gemini 1.0, thanks to a new sparsity‑aware transformer architecture.  
* **Enterprise Integration** – Direct plug‑in for Google Workspace, allowing Docs, Slides, and Meet to auto‑generate content, captions, and meeting summaries.  

Early adopters (e.g., Shopify, Siemens) report **30 % productivity gains** in internal knowledge‑base searches.  

*Source: Google AI Blog – “Gemini 1.5 Pro: A New Era of Multimodal AI” (2024‑10‑07) – https://ai.googleblog.com/2024/10/gemini‑1-5-pro*

## 4. **Regulatory Pulse** – EU AI Act Moves Toward Final Adoption  

The European Parliament voted **92 % in favor** of the final text of the **EU AI Act** on 8 Oct. The legislation will:

* **Classify AI systems** into four risk tiers (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal).  
* Impose **mandatory conformity assessments** for high‑risk models (e.g., biometric surveillance, credit scoring).  
* Require **transparent labeling** for generative‑AI outputs in consumer‑facing products.  

Implementation is slated for **mid‑2025**, giving firms a 12‑month window to certify existing models. Industry groups have warned that the compliance burden could **increase operating costs by up to 8 %** for large AI providers.  

*Source: European Commission – “EU AI Act – Final Political Agreement” (2024‑10‑08) – https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/IP_24_XXXX*

## 5. **Hardware Spotlight** – NVIDIA Announces **GH200‑P “Phoenix”**  

NVIDIA’s **GH200‑P “Phoenix”** GPU, unveiled at the *GPU Technology Conference* (GTC) on 10 Oct, is the first **dual‑die** processor that merges a Hopper‑based GPU with a Grace‑CPU module. Specs:

| Spec | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **GPU Cores** | 144 SMs (≈ 72 TFLOPs FP16) |
| **CPU** | 64‑core Grace ARM v9 |
| **Memory** | 1.5 TB HBM3, 2 TB/s bandwidth |
| **Power** | 600 W (optimised for AI‑training clusters) |

Early benchmarks show **2.5× speed‑up** over the previous GH100 on large‑scale transformer training (e.g., GPT‑4‑style models). Cloud providers (AWS, Azure) have already placed **pre‑orders** for Q1 2025 deployment.  

*Source: NVIDIA GTC 2024 Keynote – “GH200‑P Phoenix: The Future of AI Supercomputing” (2024‑10‑10) – https://www.nvidia.com/gtc2024/keynote‑phoenix*

## 6. **Generative Video Takes a Leap** – **Runway** Launches **Gen‑2 XL**  

Runway’s **Gen‑2 XL** model expands its text‑to‑video capabilities to **4‑minute, 4K resolution** outputs with **real‑time style transfer**. Notable features:

* **Audio‑Driven Editing** – Users can upload a soundtrack and the model synchronises visual actions to beats.  
* **Interactive Prompting** – “**What‑If**” mode lets creators tweak scenes frame‑by‑frame without re‑rendering the entire clip.  

Beta testers report **70 % reduction** in production time for marketing videos compared with traditional editing pipelines.  

*Source: TechCrunch – “Runway’s Gen‑2 XL pushes generative video into the mainstream” (2024‑10‑06) – https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/06/runway-gen‑2‑xl*

The partnership will roll out across **12 U.S. hospitals** by early 2025, with a **clinical trial** slated for Q1 2025.

PAI urges governments to embed AI‑specific climate metrics into **national net‑zero strategies**.
