## Weekly AI News Roundup (Nov 12‑18 2026)

The past week has been a whirlwind for the generative‑AI ecosystem. OpenAI unveiled a new model family, Google accelerated its search‑centric AI offering, and Microsoft teamed up with Salesforce to embed real‑time CRM context into its Copilot suite. Together, these moves illustrate how the industry is balancing **raw model performance**, **cost‑effective scaling**, and **deep enterprise integration**.

---

### 1. OpenAI launches GPT‑6.2 Astra and GPT‑6.2 Sol  
**Source:** *The Daily Star* – “OpenAI launches GPT‑6.2 Astra and GPT‑6.2 Sol”

- **Two‑track strategy:** OpenAI introduced a high‑end **Astra** model for professional workloads and a budget‑friendly **Sol** model for everyday developers.  
- **Pricing breakthrough:** Astra is priced at **$8 / M input tokens**, roughly **20 % cheaper** than the previous GPT‑6 Astra tier. Sol is offered at **$2 / M tokens**, opening the API to a broader set of applications.  
- **Benchmark leadership:** Both models posted top scores on the *Agents’ Last Exam* and *Science* evaluations, outpacing Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.2 and Google’s Gemini 4 variants.  
- **Availability:** Integrated across ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the OpenAI API for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Education tiers.  

*Why it matters:* By cutting prices while raising the performance ceiling, OpenAI is reinforcing its dominance in the API market and challenging rivals to match both speed and affordability.

---

### 2. Google makes Gemini 4 Hydrogen the default model in AI Mode  
**Source:** *Search Engine Journal* – “Google makes Gemini 4 Hydrogen the default model in AI Mode”

- **New default engine:** The **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** model, released on 12 Nov 2026, is now the standard for Google’s AI‑Mode search and “Tasks” feature.  
- **Speed & efficiency:** Hydrogen delivers **2.5× faster** response times than its predecessor Gemini 3.8 Flash and scores **57 %** on the Terminal‑Bench 4.1 benchmark, emphasizing throughput for high‑volume queries.  
- **Enhanced query handling:** Supports multi‑part questions, typo tolerance, and deeper follow‑up commands, pushing Google’s “agentic” search experience toward a more conversational, task‑oriented paradigm.  
- **Tiered model ecosystem:** Part of a rapid three‑model rollout (Argon, Carbon, Hydrogen) that gives developers a spectrum of pricing and capability options, reinforcing Google’s foothold in consumer‑facing generative AI.  

*Why it matters:* Google’s shift to a production‑grade, high‑throughput model signals a strategic move to keep its search platform competitive against Microsoft’s Copilot and OpenAI’s latest offerings.

---

### 3. Microsoft & Salesforce announce “Copilotforce”  
**Source:** *Microsoft Newsroom* – “Microsoft & Salesforce announce ‘Copilotforce’ – AI‑powered customer context inside Microsoft Copilot”

- **Strategic partnership:** The **Copilotforce** integration injects live Salesforce CRM data, workflows, and actions directly into Microsoft Copilot across Office, Teams, and other productivity apps.  
- **Closing the data‑silo gap:** Users can ask Copilot to retrieve a customer’s purchase history, draft follow‑up emails, or trigger a workflow without leaving the Microsoft environment.  
- **Launch context:** Announced alongside **Copilot Workspaces**, a multiplayer AI session feature unveiled at Microsoft Ignite 2026, highlighting a push toward collaborative, agentic productivity.  
- **Early adopters:** Several Fortune‑500 firms are already testing the solution, positioning it as a “trusted‑AI” layer for enterprise‑wide adoption.  

*Why it matters:* By marrying two massive enterprise ecosystems, Microsoft is setting a new standard for **data‑rich, collaborative AI** that could reshape how teams interact with CRM information in real time.
